Capture a YouTube video

Paste the video link. Then open the transcript on YouTube: under the video, click ...more in the description, then Show transcript. Select all the transcript text, copy it, and paste it below. (Level 3 automates this.)

Capture a PDF

Drop a PDF here or click to choose one. The text is pulled out right in your browser; the file itself never leaves your computer. Review the text, then save.
